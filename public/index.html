No cloud server or PhotoPort account needed

Start with a fresh CSV export from Airtable. Connecting with a read-only token is optional. One-click Airtable account authorization is not available in the local edition.

Files and task progress stay in the .runtime folder of this installation. Closing the terminal stops the service; saved files remain available for recovery and verification.

Limits: 20 MiB per file, 10 GiB and 15,000 attachments per batch. Keep at least three times the batch size free on disk.
