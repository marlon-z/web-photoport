LOCAL EDITION · v0.2.1

A few minutes to install.
Then use it on your own computer.

Start with the included product example. No Airtable account is needed until you want to export your own records.

  1. Install Node.js 22 or newer.
  2. Download and extract the source ZIP. Open a terminal in that folder.
  3. Run the two commands, then open http://127.0.0.1:4180/app.

Windows: after installing Node, you can double-click start-photoport.cmd in the extracted folder.

Before you start

Do I need R2, a database or a paid account?

No. The local edition runs on your computer. You need Node.js and enough disk space. Airtable’s own access and API limits still apply.

How do I connect my Airtable data?

Import a fresh CSV that includes official attachment download URLs. Or use a read-only personal access token limited to your chosen base. The local edition does not have hosted one-click OAuth login.

Does it change my Airtable records?

No. The export flow reads the selected data. It is not a full base backup or a two-way synchronization service.

What happens when an attachment link expires?

Import a fresh CSV of the same view with the same row and column order, then retry. Read-only API connections can refresh attachment URLs. Deleted files and removed access still need your attention.

Are my photos uploaded to PhotoPort?

No. The local tool does not send your photos or records to the maintainer. It downloads from Airtable to your computer. Saved job data remains in .runtime until you remove it.

Is the hosted version ready?

Not yet. This page offers the free local edition and an optional interest list. We are not accepting payments or promising a launch date.
