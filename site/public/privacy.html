Privacy

Updated 22 October 2026. Maintained by marlon-z / PhotoPort.

Local tool and website

The local tool processes Airtable attachments on your computer. It does not send photos, records or credentials to the maintainer. Files and task metadata remain in .runtime until you remove them. The local tool sends no telemetry and does not share its exports with this website.

What the waitlist stores

We store the email address, intended use, typical batch size, price preference, optional request and consent time you submit. These are used for PhotoPort availability and feedback contact. They are held in a private Cloudflare D1 database in the maintainer’s account, not published or sold. Do not submit photos, passwords, access keys or sensitive personal information. Signup is optional and creates no subscription or charge. Email addresses are not verified, and no email is sent automatically.

Remove your entry

Save the private removal link shown after signup. Open it and confirm to remove that submission without publicly displaying your email. Each repeated submission has its own link. The maintainer will review and remove unneeded entries at the end of the pilot. No launch date is promised.

Website traffic

The website may use free Cloudflare Web Analytics for aggregate visits. GitHub records repository and download activity. Neither is included in the local tool. To limit abuse, signup requests temporarily store a hash of the IP address combined with the date, not the raw IP. Expired limits are cleaned up during later requests. Cloudflare and GitHub process connection data under their own policies.

Contact

Contact the maintainer through the GitHub profile. Do not put email addresses, credentials or personal data in public issues. Use private vulnerability reporting for security problems. See the full data-handling notes.
